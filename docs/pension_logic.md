# Israeli Pension & Financial Logic (SSOT)

## 1. Pension Fund (קרן פנסיה)
- **Nature:** Purely a Cash Flow (תזרים) instrument upon retirement. It is an insurance/mutual-guarantee product.
- **Graph Representation:** NEVER display a Pension Fund as accumulated capital (הון צבור) on the graph.
- **Inheritance:** In case of death, it pays a survivor's pension (קצבת שאירים), it does NOT pass on as a lump sum of capital.

## 2. Executive Insurance / Provident Fund (ביטוח מנהלים / קופת גמל)
- **Nature:** A living Capital Asset (נכס הוני) that continues to accumulate yield even during retirement.
- **Usage:** Can be used to draw a monthly pension (calculated via a coefficient / מקדם) OR designated for inheritance (הורשה).
- **Graph Representation:** ALWAYS display as an accumulated capital layer on the main graph, as it remains an owned asset.
